Definition

What is Lead-Dexing?

Lead-Dexing involves deliberately publishing easily detectable content to trigger a discovery signal from a target, measure their reactivity, then establish (or not) contact.

It's a measurable discoverability game: "I mention you properly, you see it, you react (or not)".

Etymology
lead (prospect) + indexing
→ "making a lead appear on the radar"
